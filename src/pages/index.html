      Public

      General

      Public information for families and visitors.

      Note: School history, staff, accreditation, address and phone numbers are not invented here. The school's administrators will enter verified details.

      Staff only

      School Admin

      A secure, role-based dashboard.

      Security: Every admin module will check permissions on the server. Roles: Super Administrator, School Administrator, Admissions Officer, Bursary/Finance Officer, Teacher, Content Editor, Parent/Guardian.

      Teachers

      Teacher

      Tools for teachers.

      How the AI tools will work: a teacher enters the class, subject, topic and objectives, then reviews and edits the result before using it. AI output is always a draft and is never published automatically, and pupil personal data is not sent to the AI service. Report entry and resource submissions use teacher permissions inside ReportSheet and Learning Resources, so they are not listed twice.

      Registered guardians

      Parent

      A simple, private portal for guardians.

      No payment gateway: parents pay the school outside the website. The portal only collects receipts and approval requests, and a receipt is never labelled "verified" just because it was uploaded.

      Public & parents

      Learning Resources

      Approved videos, games, materials and quizzes.

      Safe content: only school-approved or properly licensed resources, moderated embeds, and no autoplay with sound.

      Build roadmap

      We build section by section.

      1. Foundation: design system, themes, EN/AR/HA + RTL, grouped home, activity slideshow
      2. Public pages: About, Academics, News, Events, Contact, Privacy
      3. Admissions: application form, uploads, tracking
      4. Backend & admin: staff sign-in, roles, audit log, admissions manager
      5. Parent portal and guardian-pupil linking
      6. Finance modules: Financial Analysis, Financial Update, ReportSheet
      7. Receipts and payment approval workflow
      8. Teacher tools: Lesson Note AI, Exam Question Generator, classes, parent messaging
      9. Learning Centre and resource manager
      10. Combine, test, harden, deploy and hand over
