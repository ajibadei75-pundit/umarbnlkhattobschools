Draft This notice is a draft. The school must review it, and obtain legal advice if needed, before the website goes live. It is written to be consistent with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and will be extended when admissions and the parent portal are added.

Who is responsible

Umar BN L Khattob Nursery and Primary School ("the school") is responsible for the information collected through this website. The school will add its official contact details on the Contact page.

What this website collects today

  • Contact form: the name, phone number or email address, topic and message you type. The form on this website prepares a message for you to send. It does not store or transmit it.
  • On your own device: your choice of light, night or system appearance and your language, plus a short counter that limits how many messages you can prepare in an hour. This stays in your browser and is not sent to anyone.

The website does not use advertising trackers.

How information is used

Information you send to the school is used to reply to your enquiry and for no other purpose. It is not sold. It is shared only with school staff who need it to respond.

Children's information

Pupil and admission records will be added in later stages with stricter protection: access limited to the people who need it, private storage and audit logs. Photographs of pupils are published only with the consent of a parent or guardian.

How long information is kept

The school will set and publish retention periods before any form stores information. Until then, nothing entered on this website is stored by the school.

Your rights

You may ask the school what information it holds about you, ask it to correct or delete that information, object to its use, and complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Changes

This notice will be updated when new features that handle personal information are added, and the date of each change will be shown here.
